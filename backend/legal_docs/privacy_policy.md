# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** January 15, 2024  
**Effective Date:** January 15, 2024  
**Version:** 1.0.0

## 1. Information We Collect

Aloft collects the following types of information to provide our flight narration services:

### 1.1 Account Information
- Email address
- User profile information
- Authentication tokens and session data
- Account creation and modification timestamps

### 1.2 Flight Data
- Flight routes and discovery history
- Session data (temporarily stored for 12 hours, then auto-deleted)
- GPS position data during active sessions
- Aircraft information (flight numbers, departure/arrival airports)

### 1.3 Generated Content
- Audio narration files
- Story text content
- Point of interest data and metadata
- User preferences and settings

### 1.4 Technical Data
- IP address and device information
- Browser type and version
- Operating system
- Referring websites
- Time zone and language preferences

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Provide flight narration services and personalized content
- Improve our content generation algorithms and service quality
- Maintain service security and prevent fraud
- Send essential service communications and updates
- Analyze usage patterns to enhance user experience
- Comply with legal obligations

## 3. Data Storage and Retention

- **Account data:** Stored until account deletion or 2 years of inactivity
- **Session data:** Automatically deleted after 12 hours
- **Generated content:** Stored for caching and future use (retained for 1 year)
- **GPS position data:** Deleted immediately after session ends
- **Technical logs:** Retained for 90 days for security purposes
- **You can request deletion of your data at any time** through account settings or by contacting privacy@aloft.app

## 4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with:

### 4.1 Service Providers
- **Groq** (for AI content generation) - processes story text content
- **ElevenLabs** (for text-to-speech) - processes text for audio generation
- **MongoDB Atlas** (database hosting) - stores application data
- **Cloudflare R2** (content delivery) - stores audio files
- **Redis Labs** (caching) - temporary session storage

### 4.2 Legal Requirements
- Law enforcement agencies when required by law
- Regulatory authorities when compelled by valid legal process
- In connection with any legal proceedings

### 4.3 Business Transfers
- In the event of merger, acquisition, or sale of assets
- With your prior consent for any other purpose

## 5. Your Rights

Under GDPR and CCPA, you have the right to:
- **Access** your personal data
- **Correct** inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Request deletion** of your data ("right to be forgotten")
- **Opt out** of data collection and marketing communications
- **Export** your data in a machine-readable format
- **Object** to processing of your personal data
- **Restrict** processing of your personal data
- **Withdraw consent** at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@aloft.app or use your account settings.

## 6. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:
- **Encryption:** AES-256 encryption for data at rest, TLS 1.3 for data in transit
- **Authentication:** Secure JWT tokens with bcrypt password hashing
- **Access Controls:** Role-based access and principle of least privilege
- **Regular Audits:** Security reviews and penetration testing
- **Rate Limiting:** Protection against brute force and abuse
- **Monitoring:** 24/7 security monitoring and incident response

## 7. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected such data, we will take steps to delete it.

## 8. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed and stored in countries other than your own. We ensure adequate protection per GDPR requirements through:
- Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) with international service providers
- EU-US Data Privacy Framework compliance where applicable
