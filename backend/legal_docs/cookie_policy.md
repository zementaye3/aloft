# Cookie Policy

**Last Updated:** January 15, 2024  
**Effective Date:** January 15, 2024  
**Version:** 1.0.0

## 1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that remember your preferences and improve your experience. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide information to website owners.

## 2. Cookies We Use

### 2.1 Essential Cookies (Required)
These cookies are necessary for the service to function and cannot be switched off:
- **Authentication tokens** - Maintain your login session
- **Session management** - Track your active flight session
- **Security features** - Prevent CSRF attacks and maintain security
- **Rate limiting** - Prevent abuse and ensure fair usage

**Lifespan:** Session-based (deleted when browser closes) or 12 hours for flight sessions

### 2.2 Functional Cookies (Optional)
These cookies enable enhanced functionality and personalization:
- **Language preferences** - Remember your preferred language
- **Display settings** - Remember UI preferences and settings
- **Route history** - Store recent flight routes for quick access
- **Audio preferences** - Remember voice and narration settings

**Lifespan:** 1 year (can be deleted in settings)

### 2.3 Analytics Cookies (Optional)
These cookies help us understand how users use the service:
- **Usage statistics** - Track feature usage and engagement
- **Performance monitoring** - Monitor service speed and reliability
- **Error tracking** - Identify and fix technical issues
- **A/B testing** - Test new features and improvements

**Lifespan:** 90 days

## 3. Third-Party Cookies

We may use third-party services that set cookies:

### 3.1 Analytics Services
- **Google Analytics** (optional) - Website and app usage analytics
- **Mixpanel** (optional) - User behavior analytics

### 3.2 Error Tracking
- **Sentry** (optional) - Error monitoring and crash reporting

### 3.3 Content Delivery
- **Cloudflare** - Content delivery and DDoS protection
- **Cloudflare R2** - Audio file delivery

## 4. Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through:

### 4.1 Browser Settings
Most browsers allow you to:
- Block all cookies
- Block third-party cookies
- Delete existing cookies
- Set cookie preferences by site

### 4.2 In-App Preferences
Our app provides cookie management options:
- Accept all cookies
- Reject non-essential cookies
- Manage specific cookie categories
- Clear all cookies and data

### 4.3 Opt-Out Links
Where available, we provide direct opt-out links for third-party analytics services.

## 5. Cookie Lifespan

- **Session cookies:** Deleted when browser closes or after 12 hours (flight sessions)
- **Persistent cookies:** Typically 1 year for preferences, 90 days for analytics
- **Authentication tokens:** Per session duration (30 minutes to 30 days depending on token type)
- **Security cookies:** Variable based on security requirements

## 6. Your Choices

You can:
- **Accept all cookies** - Full functionality and personalization
- **Reject non-essential cookies** - Core service functionality only
- **Manage cookie preferences** - Customize by category in settings
- **Clear cookies** - Remove all stored cookies and reset preferences

## 7. GDPR and Cookie Consent

### 7.1 For EU Residents
We comply with GDPR requirements for cookie consent:
- **Explicit consent** required for non-essential cookies
- **Granular consent** by cookie category
- **Easy withdrawal** of consent at any time
- **Clear explanation** of each cookie type's purpose

### 7.2 Cookie Banner
Our app displays a cookie consent banner that:
- Clearly explains cookie types and purposes
- Provides accept/reject/customize options
- Records your consent preferences
- Respects your right to withdraw consent

## 8. CCPA Compliance

For California residents under the CCPA:
- **Do Not Sell** option available for personal data
- Clear disclosure of data sharing practices
- Easy opt-out mechanism for data sales (if applicable)
- No discrimination for exercising privacy rights

*Note: We do not sell personal data as defined by the CCPA.*

## 9. Updates

We will update this policy as our use of cookies evolves. Material changes will be communicated via:
- Email notification for registered users

## 10. Technical Details

### 10.1 Cookie Types We Use
- **Session cookies** - Temporary, deleted when browser closes
- **Persistent cookies** - Remain on device until expiration or deletion
- **First-party cookies** - Set by aloft.app
- **Third-party cookies** - Set by our service providers

### 10.2 Cookie Identifiers
- **Authentication:** `auth_token`, `refresh_token`
- **Session:** `session_id`, `flight_session`
- **Preferences:** `user_prefs`, `language`, `voice_settings`
- **Analytics:** `_ga`, `_gid` (Google Analytics identifiers)
